THE IDEA

SMALL STUDIO.
BIG TASTE.

OFFCUT treats the portfolio as a cultural object rather than a list of capabilities. The visual system is simple enough to repeat, but flexible enough to change its posture from project to project.

Keep the edges visible.

Hard rules, oversized type, occasional fluorescent moments and a healthy amount of empty space create a signature without trapping the work inside a rigid brand box.
