LOUDER THAN THE FEED.

A record label identity imagined as a website pulled straight from a stack of late-night flyers. High contrast, rough edges, loud type and zero interest in looking corporate.
ART DIRECTION / WEB CONCEPTOpen full concept ↗
THE IDEA

MAKE THE NOISE
LOOK LIKE NOISE.

Instead of polishing the identity into something neutral, the system leans into the physical language of music culture: pasted posters, photocopies, venue stamps, hand-cut shapes and oversized type.

The website should feel like you found the flyer before you found the band.

The interface stays legible underneath the attitude. Releases, artists and dates have a clear hierarchy, but every surface carries enough tension to feel authored.

TYPE

BIG WORDS.
SMALL NOTES.

Condensed display lettering does the shouting. Monospace annotations handle dates, coordinates and archive details.

MOTION

MORE FLYER
THAN APP.

Hover crops, sliding labels and hard cuts create a rhythm closer to a music poster wall than a software interface.
